How to play

Fold from an edge. A short inward drag folds the outer strip (1 cell). Drag farther to fold half the board (2 cells).

Equal tiles merge into their sum; a tile folds into an empty space; different numbers block each other.

Every successful fold adds a new number. Three merges in one move earn double points. Keep going until every fold is blocked.

Tip: Use the precision buttons below the board until the gestures feel natural.
